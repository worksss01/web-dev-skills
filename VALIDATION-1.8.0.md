## Visual observations and limits

The product walkthrough uses an original generated asset, a coordinated cobalt scene, geometric display type, a separate narrow-screen image composition, material detail and a reversible visual interaction. The workspace prioritizes comparable records, context and a visible next action; its mobile layout keeps request/status together and owner detail accessible. These are the author's observations, not a universal beauty score or owner acceptance of these specific outputs.

Walkthrough HTML, generated imagery, licensed font and raw browser evidence remain local research artifacts and are not installed with the skill. The public kit contains the reusable brief/evaluation procedure. No scraped prompt collection, third-party design screenshot or private project data was added to the package.

No independent or blind baseline-versus-candidate generation comparison was performed. The other design briefs are available cases, not reported passes. Cross-browser/mobile-hardware/assistive-technology behavior, field performance and live Cowork execution were not established by these checks. Existing browser grader controls validate the grader rather than model design capability. Signatures authenticate bytes; they do not certify visual quality, security or accessibility.
